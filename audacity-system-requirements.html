Audacity system requirements for Windows — official minimum specs 2026

Audacity 3.7.3 minimum requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 4 GB RAM, 500 MB disk space, any sound card. For comfortable use, 8 GB RAM and a dedicated audio interface are recommended.

Audio requirements

What audio hardware does Audacity need?

Audacity works with any Windows sound device. For different use cases:

Basic recording
Built-in laptop or PC microphone — works, but quality is limited
Podcast / vocals
USB microphone (Blue Yeti, HyperX QuadCast) — plug and play, good quality
Music production
USB audio interface (Focusrite Scarlett, SSL 2) with XLR microphone
Vinyl digitising
USB turntable or RCA-to-USB adapter into audio interface
Loopback recording
Sound card with WASAPI loopback support — most modern cards qualify
FAQ

Requirements questions

Audacity system requirements 2026 — what are the official specs?

The official Audacity 3.7 requirements are: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), minimum 4 GB RAM, 500 MB disk space. In practice, large multi-track projects need 8+ GB RAM. The official requirements are published at support.audacityteam.org.

Does Audacity work on Windows 10?

Yes. Audacity 3.7.3 fully supports Windows 10 64-bit. Both Windows 10 and Windows 11 are officially supported. 32-bit Windows is no longer supported as of Audacity 3.0.

Audacity minimum requirements for multitrack recording

For multitrack recording with many effects: 8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB recommended. Multi-track projects with 10+ tracks and real-time effects processing can use 2–4 GB RAM. SSD storage is important for low-latency recording of multiple simultaneous tracks.
