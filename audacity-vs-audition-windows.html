Audacity vs Adobe Audition on Windows — which to choose in 2026

Audacity is free and open source. Adobe Audition is a paid professional DAW ($54.99/month). Audacity covers recording, editing and basic production. Audition adds advanced multitrack, spectral repair, auto-ducking and deep Creative Cloud integration.

Choose Audacity when

When Audacity is the better choice

  • Budget — Audacity is completely free with no subscriptions
  • Podcasting and voice recording — Audacity handles all standard podcast workflows
  • Learning audio — simpler interface, huge free tutorial ecosystem
  • Linux — Audacity runs on Linux; Audition does not
FAQ

Comparison questions

Is Audacity good enough for professional podcasting?

Yes. Many professional podcasters use Audacity. It handles recording, noise reduction, level adjustment, editing and MP3 export — everything a podcast workflow needs. The main limitation vs Audition is batch processing and advanced noise reduction for difficult recordings.

Can I import Adobe Audition projects into Audacity?

Not directly. Audition uses a proprietary project format (.sesx). Export individual tracks as WAV from Audition and import them into Audacity. You lose automation and effects but preserve the audio.
